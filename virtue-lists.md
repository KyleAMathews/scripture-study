# Virtue Lists in Scripture

This note gathers passages that explicitly list virtues or attributes. Each entry names the list and links to the local verse file.

## Old Testament
- Micah 6:8: do justly, love mercy, walk humbly with God. ([Micah 6:8](standard-works-vault/Old%20Testament/Micah%206.8.md))
- Isaiah 11:2: wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge, fear of the Lord. ([Isaiah 11:2](standard-works-vault/Old%20Testament/Isaiah%2011.2.md))

## New Testament
- Beatitudes: poor in spirit, they that mourn, meek, hunger and thirst after righteousness, merciful, pure in heart, peacemakers, persecuted for righteousness. ([Matthew 5:3](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Matthew%205.3.md)) ([Matthew 5:12](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Matthew%205.12.md))
- Fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance. ([Galatians 5:22](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Galatians%205.22.md)) ([Galatians 5:23](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Galatians%205.23.md))
- Add to your faith: virtue, knowledge, temperance, patience, godliness, brotherly kindness, charity. ([2 Peter 1:5](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/2%20Peter%201.5.md)) ([2 Peter 1:7](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/2%20Peter%201.7.md))
- Charity attributes: suffers long, kind, envies not, not puffed up, seeks not her own, not easily provoked, thinks no evil, rejoices in truth, bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things. ([1 Corinthians 13:4](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/1%20Corinthians%2013.4.md)) ([1 Corinthians 13:7](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/1%20Corinthians%2013.7.md))
- Abiding triad: faith, hope, charity. ([1 Corinthians 13:13](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/1%20Corinthians%2013.13.md))
- Think on these things: true, honest, just, pure, lovely, good report, virtue, praise. ([Philippians 4:8](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Philippians%204.8.md))
- Put on as the elect of God: mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering, forbearing, forgiving, charity. ([Colossians 3:12](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Colossians%203.12.md)) ([Colossians 3:13](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Colossians%203.13.md)) ([Colossians 3:14](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Colossians%203.14.md))
- Wisdom from above: pure, peaceable, gentle, easy to be entreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, without hypocrisy. ([James 3:17](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/James%203.17.md))
- Follow after: righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness. ([1 Timothy 6:11](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/1%20Timothy%206.11.md))
- Flee youthful lusts; follow: righteousness, faith, charity, peace. ([2 Timothy 2:22](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/2%20Timothy%202.22.md))

## Book of Mormon
- Beatitudes: poor in spirit who come unto Christ, they that mourn, meek, hunger and thirst after righteousness, merciful, pure in heart, peacemakers, persecuted for righteousness. ([3 Nephi 12:3](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/3%20Nephi%2012.3.md)) ([3 Nephi 12:12](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/3%20Nephi%2012.12.md))
- Mosiah 3:19: submissive, meek, humble, patient, full of love, willing to submit. ([Mosiah 3:19](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/Mosiah%203.19.md))
- Alma 7:23: humble, submissive, gentle, easy to be entreated, patient, long-suffering, temperate, diligent, thankful. ([Alma 7:23](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/Alma%207.23.md))
- Alma 7:24: faith, hope, charity. ([Alma 7:24](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/Alma%207.24.md))
- Alma 13:28: humble, meek, submissive, patient, full of love, long-suffering. ([Alma 13:28](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/Alma%2013.28.md))
- 2 Nephi 31:20: steadfastness in Christ, hope, love of God and all men, endure to the end. ([2 Nephi 31:20](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/2%20Nephi%2031.20.md))
- Moroni 7:45: charity suffers long, is kind, envies not, not puffed up, seeks not her own, not easily provoked, thinks no evil, rejoices in truth, bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things. ([Moroni 7:45](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/Moroni%207.45.md))

## Doctrine and Covenants
- D&C 4:5: faith, hope, charity, love, eye single to the glory of God. ([D&C 4:5](standard-works-vault/Doctrine%20and%20Covenants/D&C%204.5.md))
- D&C 4:6: faith, virtue, knowledge, temperance, patience, brotherly kindness, godliness, charity, humility, diligence. ([D&C 4:6](standard-works-vault/Doctrine%20and%20Covenants/D&C%204.6.md))
- D&C 6:19: patient, sober, temperate, patience, faith, hope, charity. ([D&C 6:19](standard-works-vault/Doctrine%20and%20Covenants/D&C%206.19.md))
- D&C 121:41-42: persuasion, long-suffering, gentleness, meekness, love unfeigned, kindness, pure knowledge, without hypocrisy, without guile. ([D&C 121:41](standard-works-vault/Doctrine%20and%20Covenants/D&C%20121.41.md)) ([D&C 121:42](standard-works-vault/Doctrine%20and%20Covenants/D&C%20121.42.md))

## Pearl of Great Price
- Articles of Faith 1:13: honest, true, chaste, benevolent, virtuous, do good to all; anything virtuous, lovely, good report, praiseworthy. ([Articles of Faith 1:13](standard-works-vault/Pearl%20of%20Great%20Price/Articles%20of%20Faith%201.13.md))

## Connections and echoes
- Faith, hope, charity appear together in both the New Testament and Book of Mormon. ([1 Corinthians 13:13](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/1%20Corinthians%2013.13.md)) ([Alma 7:24](standard-works-vault/Book%20of%20Mormon/Alma%207.24.md))
- The 2 Peter ladder of virtue is echoed in D&C 4. ([2 Peter 1:5](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/2%20Peter%201.5.md)) ([D&C 4:6](standard-works-vault/Doctrine%20and%20Covenants/D&C%204.6.md))
- Philippians 4:8 and Articles of Faith 1:13 share the same "virtue, lovely, good report" vocabulary. ([Philippians 4:8](standard-works-vault/New%20Testament/Philippians%204.8.md)) ([Articles of Faith 1:13](standard-works-vault/Pearl%20of%20Great%20Price/Articles%20of%20Faith%201.13.md))
