# Round 6: Determinate Negation — Discernment vs. Hiddenness

## Pre-Synthesis Guess

"Recognition operates on a delay — the encounter is opaque during the trial but becomes legible afterward, and the accumulated experience of past recognitions builds the capacity for faster recognition in future encounters. Discernment is real but developmental, not immediate."

## 5.0 Internal Tensions (Self-Sublation)

### Monk A (Discernment) Undermines Himself

1. **The "by reason of use" concession.** Recognition is structurally inevitable (same substance = automatic resonance) — but it sharpens through practice (Hebrews 5:14). If automatic, why does it need development? The need for training undermines the inevitability claim.

2. **The noise problem.** The problem is always noise, never absent signal. But if noise drowns the signal, the encounter is functionally opaque for the person in the noise. "The signal was always there" is unfalsifiable metaphysics — the same retreat Monk A (Unity) made in Round 5.

3. **"No unrecognized encounter" vs. the pastoral crisis.** The pastoral crisis that motivated this dialectic consists of encounters that WERE NOT recognized. If every encounter announces itself, the crisis doesn't exist. But it does.

### Monk B (Hiddenness) Undermines Himself

1. **The contact concession.** The Spirit provides contact — awareness God is present. But contact IS partial recognition. The absolute opacity claim is already compromised by his own distinction.

2. **The Helaman 10 problem.** If opacity doesn't diminish across the spiral, the telos is impossible. Will-convergence requires some recognition of what God wants. Permanent opacity makes the encounter-spiral incoherent.

3. **Jill contradicts the extreme claim.** If opacity is permanent for mortality, Jill should never have understood "myopic." But she did — "that word now fills me with peace." His best example is a delayed recognition story, not a permanent opacity story.

## 5.1 Surface Contradiction

**Monk A:** "The encounter is recognizable — Spirit recognizes Spirit, the signal is self-disclosing."

**Monk B:** "The encounter is opaque — the person can't see their own condition, and the condition determines the form."

## 5.2 Shared Assumptions

1. **Recognition is binary** — recognizable or opaque, with no middle ground.
2. **Recognition is a property of the moment** — either you see it now or you don't. Neither asks whether recognition is designed to operate ACROSS TIME.
3. **Recognition is individual** — both locate it internally. Neither considers communal recognition.
4. **The purpose of recognition is comprehension** — understanding what God is doing and why. Neither asks whether recognition serves a different purpose: ORIENTATION rather than comprehension.

**Most important: #2.** The Packer/Lee quote directly challenges it: "Walk to the edge of the light, then a few steps into the darkness; then the light will appear."

## 5.3 Determinate Negation

**Monk A fails** because his recognition is too immediate and too complete. If the encounter announces itself through Spirit-resonance, Paul should have known what God was doing with the thorn. Joseph shouldn't have cried "where art thou?" His retreat to "he knew God was speaking, just not the form" is a massive concession — recognition that covers only contact, not pastoral content. **Missing:** an account of what IS recognizable, when, and why that portion is sufficient.

**Monk B fails** because his opacity is too total and too permanent. The encounter-spiral requires increasing recognition — otherwise there's no mechanism for will-convergence. Jill's story is delayed recognition, not permanent opacity. **Missing:** an account of how the rhythm of opacity-and-recognition IS the mechanism of growth.

## 5.4 The Hidden Question

Both ask: "Is the encounter recognizable or opaque?"

**Hidden question:** What is the temporal structure of recognition in the encounter?

Monk A: now. Monk B: never. Ether 12:6: AFTER. Packer/Lee: "a few steps into the darkness; THEN the light will appear." Recognition is SEQUENTIAL.

## 5.5 Boydian Decomposition

### Cross-Domain Connections

**A. "Sharpens by use" + "witness comes after" = the same phenomenon.** What is the "use" that sharpens discernment? Retrospective recognition. You step into darkness, light appears afterward, that experience calibrates for the next step. The rhythm IS the training.

**B. Contact ≠ comprehension + Nephi's dual knowledge = recognition sufficient for ORIENTATION.** "I know he loveth his children; nevertheless, I do not know the meaning of all things." Enough to orient, not enough to predict or control. Recognition is sufficient for orientation, insufficient for comprehension.

**C. "Light compounds" + "person can't see own condition" = the spiral produces TRUST, not transparency.** What grows isn't the ability to see your condition — it's the ability to trust the encounter without needing to see. Helaman 10's Nephi doesn't comprehend everything — he trusts so completely that comprehension is unnecessary.

**D. D&C 46 communal gifts + all individual accounts = recognition is designed to be communally mediated.** Discernment is distributed across the community. The "lower light" in Packer's boat story was a person on the shore. Recognition isn't purely internal — other people can see what you can't.

### Adjacent Domain Material: The Packer Talk

"Walk to the edge of the light, then a few steps into the darkness; then the light will appear and show the way before you."

The temporal structure:
1. ORIENTATION: See enough to know where the edge is (contact — Monk A's territory)
2. RISK: Step beyond what you can see (opacity — Monk B's territory)
3. RECOGNITION: Light appears after the step (Ether 12:6's "after")
4. CALIBRATION: New light becomes the new edge (D&C 50:24's compounding)

The Samoa boat story adds the communal dimension: the upper light (God) always burns. The lower light (human mediator) helps find the passage through the reef. When the lower light fails, you're lost in the storm. "Let the lower lights be burning."

### Emergent Structure

The rhythmic cycle (orientation → risk → recognition → calibration) and the communal mediation ("lower lights") are in neither monk's input. The rhythm explains both monks: Monk A perceives the recognition phases; Monk B perceives the opacity phases. Each absolutizes one phase of the cycle.

## 5.6 Sublation Criteria

- **Preserve from A:** Recognition is real. The Light of Christ works. Resonance is valid. The encounter IS designed to be recognized.
- **Preserve from B:** Opacity is real. The person can't see their own condition. Faith requires acting without full sight. Opacity is theologically necessary.
- **Dissolve:** The binary framing. The assumption that recognition is individual and simultaneous.
- **Answer:** Recognition is sequential and rhythmic — opaque in the moment of risk, luminous after the step of faith. It is communally mediated through "lower lights." The telos of the rhythm is trust (orientation + willingness to act in darkness), not transparency (comprehension of what God is doing).
